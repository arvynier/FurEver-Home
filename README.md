<b><i>FurEver Home</i></b> is a Java-based Animal Adoption and Responsible Ownership System designed to facilitate the adoption process while promoting informed and responsible pet ownership. It supports the whole adoption process from posting an animal for adoption to completing a responsible adoption. Specifically, FurEver Home promotes responsible, planned adoption by assessing an adopter's ability to care for the animal they intend to adopt and providing information such as the long-term cost of pet ownership. As such, it ensures that animals are adopted by people who are prepared and committed to care for them for life. 

<h2 align="left">
  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.herokuapp.com?font=Press+Start+2P&size=12&pause=1000&color=87CEEB&center=false&vCenter=true&width=600&lines=Problem+being+Addressed" />
</h2>
Some adopters may adopt on impulse or lack sufficient financial resources, time, knowledge, or a suitable living environment to properly care for an animal. Shelters also lack a standardized and efficient process of assessing the adopters’ readiness. As such, this can lead to neglected pets, unsuccessful adoptions, animals being returned to shelters and rescuers, or animal suffering.  

FurEver Home addresses this problem by providing an adoption platform that not only connects animals with potential adopters but also encourages responsible and planned adoption through readiness assessments, animal care information, and estimated long-term ownership costs. Thus, this system aims to ensure that these animals up for adoption find their forever home. 

<h2 align="left">
  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.herokuapp.com?font=Press+Start+2P&size=12&pause=1000&color=87CEEB&center=false&vCenter=true&width=600&lines=Problem+Objectives" />
</h2>
FurEver Home aims to promote responsible animal adoption by assessing the adopter's capability and readiness to adopt an animal, and informing them of the long-term cost and commitment of caring for the animal they want to adopt. 

Specifically, this project aims to:  

1.  Provide a centralized platform where users can browse animals that are ready for adoption.

2. Provide detailed animal profiles to help prospective adopters understand each animal’s needs and condition.
   
3. Evaluate prospective adopters’ readiness, financial capacity, and ability to provide proper animal care. 

4. Help the administrators assess adoption applications before making approval decisions. 

5. Establish communication between adopters and animal poster or rescuers. 

6. Promote responsible pet ownership by providing care guides and information about long-term expenses.

  <img src="https://readme-typing-svg.herokuapp.com?font=Press+Start+2P&size=12&pause=1000&color=87CEEB&center=false&vCenter=true&width=600&lines=Target+Users" />
</h2>
<b>Prospective Adopter</b> – individual that is interested in adopting animals. They can browse the animal profiles, submit an adoption application, take readiness assessment, and read long-term costs and care guidelines. 

<b>Current Owner/Rescuer</b> – posting animals for adoption or rehoming. They can create and manage animal listings, provide animal information, respond to interested adopters, and approve/reject adoption applications.
